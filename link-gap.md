## Later: when you have clients and reviews

- **10SEOs** (10seos.com): links to 3 of 4. SEO agency rankings.
- **The Org** (theorg.com): links to 4 of 4. Company team page; worth it once there is a team.
- **Forbes and HuffPost**: 2 competitors each. Usually paid councils or contributor spots. Paid membership links should not be treated as earned links.

## Skipped on purpose

- **Glassdoor, Lusha, ContactOut and similar**: job and contact-data sites, not worth chasing.
- **Throwaway spam sites** (.pages.dev pages, "site value" checkers): same kind of junk that already links to you.
- **Built In**: a tech employer site that needs job postings.
