## Step 3 · Their answer decides your next message

**They say no, not sure, or not as much as they'd like**
```
That's really common for [plumbing/HVAC/roofing] companies, and it's usually fixable. I'm doing free written audits right now: your website, Google Business Profile and how you show up on Google, Maps and AI search, with the top fixes first. No cost and no obligation.

Want me to do one for [Business name]?
```

**They say yes, they show up**
```
Nice, that's a good spot to be in. Is most of your work coming from Google right now, or more from referrals and repeat customers?
```

Then, only if they sound open to more Google work:
```
Makes sense. If it'd help, I can do a free written audit to spot anything that would pick up more of those searches. No cost and no obligation. Want one?
```

**They say yes to the audit**
```
Great! Just fill this out and I'll get started: https://provoseopros.com/free-seo-audit/

It takes about a minute, and no logins are needed.
```

**No reply after they accepted** (one follow-up after 4 or 5 days, then stop)
```
Hi [First name], no worries if you're slammed with jobs. If a free audit of how [Business name] shows up on Google and Maps would ever help, just let me know.
```

## Moving people through GoHighLevel

- **Contacted:** you sent the first message after they accepted
- **Said yes:** they want the audit (the form adds them on its own once the workflow step is added)
- **Audit delivered:** you sent the report. The review ask goes out 2 days later and the referral ask 5 days later, on their own
- **Not now:** they declined

The review ask is never part of the audit offer. It only comes after delivery, and it's always optional.
